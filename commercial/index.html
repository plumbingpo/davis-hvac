Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Davis HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule visits around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Every hour without climate control costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Davis HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Davis and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.
